Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Password Manager, developed by Aditya Ambre, handles information when you use the application.

Password Manager is designed as an offline password-management application. Your password-manager data is intended to remain stored locally on your device unless you explicitly choose to export, back up, or otherwise share that data outside the application.

2. Information We Collect

Password Manager does not require you to create an account with Aditya Ambre to use the core password-management functionality.

Information entered into the application, such as saved usernames, passwords, organization names, notes, URLs, and other credential information, is stored locally on your device.

Aditya Ambre does not operate a server that receives or stores your password-manager credentials.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

Password Manager is designed to operate primarily on your device. Subject to the operation of third-party services described in this Privacy Policy, Aditya Ambre does not receive or collect the following information through the application's password-management functionality:

  • Your stored passwords and other password-manager credentials.
  • Your master password.
  • Your PIN used for local application authentication.
  • Your biometric information, such as fingerprints or face data.
  • Your locally created backup files.
  • Your payment card or other payment details used for purchases.

Payment transactions and purchase processing are handled by Google Play and are subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

4. Local Storage

Password-manager information is stored locally on your device using the application's local storage/database system.

The application does not upload your stored credentials to a Aditya Ambre server.

Because your password-manager data is stored locally, you are responsible for maintaining access to your device and keeping your device, authentication credentials, and locally stored data secure.

5. Backup & Restore

Password Manager may provide functionality to create and restore backups of your password-manager data.

Backup files are created locally based on your actions and may be saved to a location selected by you. Aditya Ambre does not receive or store these backup files on its own servers.

You are responsible for protecting any backup file you create, including maintaining its confidentiality and storing it in a secure location.

The application's password-protected backup file uses the .pmb file extension. You must remember the password you set for the backup to restore it. If you lose that password, the backup may not be recoverable.

Passkeys stored by Password Manager remain on your device and are not included in .pmb backup files. Do not assume that passkeys can be restored from a backup.

If you transfer, copy, upload, or otherwise share a backup file outside the application, the handling of that file is subject to the security and privacy practices of the destination or service you choose.

Important: Do not share your backup files with untrusted individuals or services if they contain sensitive password-manager information.

6. Data Encryption & Security

Password Manager is designed to protect sensitive password-manager data using encryption before it is stored locally on the device.

The encryption key is derived from your master password using PBKDF2 with a unique salt. The derived encryption key is generated when required and is not permanently stored by the application.

Password Manager uses AES-256-GCM encryption to protect sensitive stored password-manager data.

Security Notice: Aditya Ambre takes reasonable measures to protect your data; however, no software, device, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Local Authentication

Password Manager may provide local authentication options, including PIN-based authentication and biometric authentication supported by your device.

These authentication methods are used locally on your device to control access to the application.

Aditya Ambre does not receive or store your device's biometric data. Biometric verification is handled by the authentication mechanisms provided by your device's operating system.

8. Master Password

Your master password is used locally by the application to derive the encryption key used to protect your password-manager data.

Aditya Ambre does not receive or store your master password.

Because the master password is an important part of protecting your encrypted data, you should keep it confidential and secure.

Important: If you forget your master password, access to data protected by that password may not be recoverable. Do not share your master password with anyone.

9. Passwords, Passkeys & 2-FA Authenticators

Passkeys managed by Password Manager are stored on your device. They are not included in .pmb backup files. Passkey availability and use may also depend on the supported platform and the service or website with which the passkey is used.

The application can store authenticator information for TOTP (Time-based One-Time Password) and HOTP (HMAC-based One-Time Password) verification codes. These authenticator details are managed locally in connection with the relevant saved credential.

On Android, you may select Password Manager as your Autofill service to fill saved login information in supported apps and websites. Autofill availability and behavior depend on Android, the target app or website, and its support for Autofill.

10. Internet Access

The core password-management functionality of Password Manager is designed to work offline and does not require your password-manager data to be transmitted to Aditya Ambre.

Certain features, such as Google Play purchase processing or other third-party services, may require an internet connection.

11. In-App Purchases

Password Manager may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Aditya Ambre does not receive or store your payment card details or other payment credentials used to complete a Google Play purchase.

Information associated with purchases may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy policy and terms.

12. Third-Party Services

Password Manager may use third-party services required for certain application functionality, such as Google Play services for distributing the application and processing in-app purchases.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. Aditya Ambre does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

The application may use Firebase Analytics to record app usage events, such as screen visits, and Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnostic information. When these services are enabled and a network connection is available, relevant event, crash, and diagnostic data may be transmitted to Firebase for analytics and troubleshooting. This information is handled under Google's/Firebase's applicable terms and privacy documentation. These services are separate from the local password vault; this does not mean that your saved passwords or passkeys are uploaded to those services.

13. Data Sharing

Aditya Ambre does not sell, rent, or otherwise share your password-manager credentials with third parties.

Your locally stored password-manager data remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export, back up, transfer, or otherwise share it.

Third-party services, such as Google Play, may independently process information necessary to provide their services.

14. Data Deletion

You can delete password-manager information using the deletion functionality provided within the application.

Data stored locally by the application may also be removed when application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of your device's operating system.

Backup files created outside the application's internal storage are not automatically deleted when you remove application data or uninstall the application. You are responsible for deleting those backup files if you no longer want to retain them.

15. Children's Privacy

Password Manager is not specifically directed toward children. Aditya Ambre does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application's password-management functionality.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
